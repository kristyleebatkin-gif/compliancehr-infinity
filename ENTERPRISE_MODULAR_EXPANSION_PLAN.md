## Phase 1 — Compliance Foundation
Dependencies: Foundation acceptance

### Compliance OS
Registers: Standards, Practice Standards, Compliance Obligations, Legislative, Regulatory, Control, Compliance Assessments
Features: Standards mapping; Obligation management; Compliance monitoring; Control management; Compliance scoring; Compliance dashboards; AI compliance guidance

### Evidence Intelligence OS
Registers: Evidence, Evidence Ownership, Evidence Relationship, Evidence Collection
Features: Central evidence repository; Evidence scoring; Evidence confidence ratings; Evidence traceability; Evidence expiry monitoring; Evidence lineage mapping; Evidence relationships

### Policy & Document Intelligence OS
Registers: Policy, Procedure, Form, Template, Guidelines
Features: Version control; Approval workflows; Review scheduling; Policy acknowledgements; AI content generation; Policy mapping to standards; Automatic review reminders

Executive indicators: Compliance Score, Evidence Score, Obligations Status, Policy Review Status, Risk Exposure Summary

Acceptance: Reconcile obligation–control–evidence relationships, scoring denominators, evidence versions and policy approval lifecycle. Verify the five executive indicators with authorised real records.

## Release gates
- Reconcile existing entities, routes and evidence links before adding resources.
- Verify approved-role access and tenant isolation on the server, including denied access.
- Verify a complete workflow with exact evidence versions, provenance and approval authority.
- Test loading, empty, error, export, mobile and keyboard behaviour.
- Record acceptance evidence and a restorable checkpoint; record publication separately.

## Target end state
25+ enterprise operating systems, 350+ core entity models, unified evidence graph, enterprise data warehouse, 356-checkpoint audit framework, multi-tenant architecture and enterprise AI. Counts are targets; create no duplicate entities to reach a number. Security, privacy, audit logging and AI governance begin in the foundation. Framework editions and regulatory obligations require authoritative validation before implementation. Router Remote is an unresolved navigation requirement; preserve the existing React Router until its contract is clarified.
