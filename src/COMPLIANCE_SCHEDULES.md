# NDIS Compliance Schedule & Registers (owner-confirmed, 2026-10-22)

Reference list shown on every employee and participant profile via the
"Compliance & Audit Requirements" panel (ProfileComplianceRequirements).

## Cadences
- **6-monthly:** first aid kit checks, fire safety equipment and extinguisher
  checks, fire and evacuation drills, MSDS reviews, natural disaster kit
  checks. Emergency plans reviewed and kept accessible.
- **3-monthly:** internal audits, documented with stored evidence and
  corrective actions. The same documented evidence and corrective actions
  apply to incidents, feedback and risks.
- **Daily:** cleaning checklist, sign in and out register.
- **Ongoing:** infection control, waste management, PPE, manual handling,
  continuous improvement.
- **On file, kept current:** business structure, strategic plan, operations
  plan, business plan, emergency plans.

## Registers that must be kept
- Document register (every document recorded)
- Legal requirements register (acknowledgements)
- Human resource register (list of every employee)
- Service delivery register (all participants)
- Incident, risk and feedback registers (all entries recorded)
- Continuous improvement register
- Sign in and out register

## Actions, workload and manager copies (owner-confirmed, 2026-10-22)
- Compliance tasks are sent as **to-do tasks** and **allocated to staff**, so the
  workload is shared and completion is itself recorded evidence.
- Every action taken is recorded on completion as evidence and corrective action.
- **All meetings must be documented** (minutes, attendees, actions).
- **Managers receive a copy of all**: incidents, risks, issues, concerns, tasks,
  corrective actions, feedback and follow-ups. Most need a follow-up review
  completed and recorded.
- **Built (2026-10-22):** every incident, risk (any rating) and feedback is
  auto-routed to a manager copy the moment it is recorded — a Notification to
  admin, Provider Administrator, Compliance Manager and Administration users,
  linked to the record, plus its register entry.

## NDIS registration audits
Evidence pack and registration groups kept audit-ready, drawing on the
safety checks, drills, audits, registers and business documents above.

## Provider policy update — 29 October 2026
- Employee and participant profiles: review every **3 calendar months** from the last completed review. A reminder or request does not count as a completed review.
- Staff training: renewal due every **12 calendar months** from the evidenced completion date. Assignment, attendance, assessment and practical competency are distinct states.
- Missing completion evidence means the due date is unknown; do not infer current training or invent a completion date.
- Preserve externally issued qualification expiry dates. Earlier credential expiry, participant-specific requirements or identified competency gaps require earlier action.
- These intervals are the provider's operating policy, not a claim of a universal statutory renewal interval.
- Reminder delivery, escalation and end-to-end renewal tracking require validation before acceptance. Updating a displayed schedule or module default does not prove a reminder was delivered.

## Shift and home safety policy — 29 October 2026
- Shift notes are due after every shift. Include actual supports, goals/progress, concerns, incidents and handover. Urgent incidents must be escalated immediately.
- Daily employee reminders on days 1–5 after shift end; unresolved notes pass to management from day 6. Current scheduled sweep: 19:00 Australia/Sydney. A reminder is not completion evidence.
- Car safety check every shift, linked to that shift; defects require prompt escalation and controls.
- Home risk assessment every calendar month, with assessment evidence, controls, owner and next-due date; earlier review after material changes.
- Remaining validation: CommunicationEntry/ShiftNote reconciliation, exact linkage of multiple same-day shifts, enabled schedules, delivery, manager follow-up and monthly participant/home coverage.
