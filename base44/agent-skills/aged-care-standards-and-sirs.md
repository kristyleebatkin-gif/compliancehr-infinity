# Aged care: strengthened Quality Standards and SIRS

Last checked: 2 November 2026.
Sources: Department of Health "Strengthened Aged Care Quality Standards" (November 2025); Aged Care Quality and Safety Commission "About reportable incidents" and "Reportable incidents and SIRS - quick guide to changes from 1 November 2025".

## Strengthened Aged Care Quality Standards (from 1 November 2025)
Commenced with the Aged Care Act 2024 and the Aged Care Rules 2025. The outcomes are legal obligations that apply depending on the services a provider delivers. Standards 6 and 7 apply to residential care homes.
1. **The Individual** - 1.1 person-centred care; 1.2 dignity, respect and privacy; 1.3 choice, independence and quality of life; 1.4 transparency and agreements.
2. **The Organisation** - 2.1 partnering with individuals; 2.2 quality, safety and inclusion culture; 2.3 accountability, quality system, policies and procedures; 2.4 risk management; 2.5 incident management; 2.6 complaints and feedback management; 2.7 information management; 2.8 workforce planning; 2.9 human resource management; 2.10 emergency and disaster management.
3. **The Care and Services** - 3.1 assessment and planning; 3.2 delivery of funded aged care services; 3.3 communicating for safety and quality; 3.4 planning and coordination.
4. **The Environment** - 4.1 environment (in the home, or elsewhere); 4.2 infection prevention and control.
5. **Clinical Care** - 5.1 clinical governance; 5.2 infection prevention in clinical care; 5.3 safe and quality use of medicines; 5.4 comprehensive care; 5.5 safety of clinical care services; 5.6 cognitive impairment; 5.7 palliative and end-of-life care.
6. **Food and Nutrition** (residential) - 6.1 partnering on food and drinks; 6.2 assessing nutritional needs; 6.3 provision of food and drinks; 6.4 dining experience.
7. **The Residential Community** (residential) - 7.1 daily living; 7.2 transitions.

## Serious Incident Response Scheme (SIRS)
Covers reportable incidents connected to the care of an older person receiving funded aged care services, including in residential care and in the home.

Eight reportable incident types:
1. Unreasonable use of force.
2. Unlawful sexual contact or inappropriate sexual conduct.
3. Psychological or emotional abuse.
4. Unexpected death.
5. Stealing or financial coercion by a staff member.
6. Neglect.
7. Inappropriate use of restrictive practices.
8. Unexplained absence from care.

Priority and timeframes (from when the provider becomes aware):
- **Priority 1 - notify the Commission within 24 hours.** Includes incidents that caused, or could reasonably have caused, physical or psychological injury or discomfort needing medical or psychological treatment; unlawful sexual contact or inappropriate sexual conduct; unexpected death; unexplained absence from care; and incidents where there are reasonable grounds to report to police.
- **Priority 2 - notify within 30 days.** All other reportable incidents.
- **Police:** where there are reasonable grounds to suspect a crime, notify police within 24 hours.

The Aged Care Act 2024 and Aged Care Rules 2025 changed some SIRS requirements from 1 November 2025. The details are in the Commission's SIRS provider guidelines; recommend checking them for which registration categories apply.

## How to answer
- Map facts to the eight SIRS types and decide Priority 1 or 2; if details are missing, say what is needed.
- Link quality questions to the relevant standard and outcome number, and the evidence an assessor would expect.
- Use "older person", matching current Commission language.
- Never give legal advice and never say a provider is compliant.
