# NDIS Practice Standards and Code of Conduct

Last checked: 2 November 2026.
Sources: NDIS Commission Practice Standards pages and "NDIS Practice Standards and Quality Indicators"; Explanatory document for the NDIS (Provider Registration and Practice Standards) Amendment (Mandatory Registration and Other Matters) Rules 2026; NDIS Code of Conduct.

## Practice Standards - structure
Registered providers are audited against the NDIS Practice Standards and Quality Indicators:
- **Core Module** (applies to most registered providers, assessed by certification audit), covering:
  - Rights and responsibilities (person-centred supports, individual values and beliefs, privacy and dignity, independence and informed choice, violence, abuse, neglect, exploitation and discrimination).
  - Governance and operational management (governance, risk management, quality management, information management, feedback and complaints, incident management, human resource management, continuity of supports, emergency and disaster management).
  - Provision of supports (access to supports, support planning, service agreements, responsive support provision, transitions).
  - Support provision environment (safe environment, participant money and property, management of medication, mealtime management, waste management).
- **Supplementary modules** for specific supports, for example: high intensity daily personal activities; specialist behaviour support; implementing behaviour support plans; early childhood supports; specialised support coordination; specialist disability accommodation.
- **Verification module** for lower-risk supports.

## 2026 changes
- **Supported independent living (SIL):** SIL providers must be registered and meet new SIL Practice Standards plus the Core Module from 1 July 2026. Unregistered providers already delivering SIL had until 1 October 2026 to apply.
- **Digital platform providers:** mandatory registration; must meet the Core Module and only allow workers with a worker screening clearance. Platform-specific conditions apply from 1 January 2027.
- **Change of ownership:** new conditions and, for some providers, an ownership-change audit, from 1 July 2026.
- **Faster notifications:** certain events and changes must be notified to the Commissioner sooner.
- A wider Practice Standards Review is under way; no commencement date has been announced. Check the Commission's Practice Standards page for updates.

## NDIS Code of Conduct - seven elements
Applies to all NDIS providers (registered and unregistered) and their workers. Workers must:
1. Act with respect for individual rights to freedom of expression, self-determination and decision-making.
2. Respect the privacy of people with disability.
3. Provide supports and services in a safe and competent manner, with care and skill.
4. Act with integrity, honesty and transparency.
5. Promptly take steps to raise and act on concerns about matters that might have an impact on the quality and safety of supports.
6. Take all reasonable steps to prevent and respond to all forms of violence, exploitation, neglect and abuse.
7. Take all reasonable steps to prevent and respond to sexual misconduct.

## How to answer
- Link the question to the relevant standard and suggest the evidence an auditor would expect (policy, procedure, records, training, review dates).
- Point to records in ComplianceHR (policies, training completions, incidents, complaints, risk register, internal audits) as evidence.
- Say when something changed recently and recommend checking the Commission's current version.
- Never give legal advice and never say a provider is compliant or audit-ready as a fact.
